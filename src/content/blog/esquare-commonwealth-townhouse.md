When most people think of Esquare Estates, they think of Bulacan — flood-free lots, McArthur Highway, the Philippine Arena corridor. But the brand's roots go deeper. **Esquare Estates Commonwealth**, developed by **Metrostar Realty & Development Inc.**, is a premium townhouse project tucked inside one of Quezon City's most established private villages: **Ciudad Regina**.

This is not a mass-market development. There are only two units. And that exclusivity is exactly the point.

## What Is Esquare Estates Commonwealth?

Commonwealth is an intimate, two-unit enclave of **modern contemporary townhouses** designed with a sleek minimalist aesthetic — clean lines, a refined palette of light hues, and a layout built for how Filipinos actually live. Two floors, four bedrooms, four toilets and baths, and a two-car carport per unit.

The architecture avoids the boxy, cookie-cutter look that dominates most Philippine townhouse developments. Instead, the façade reads more like a premium residential design you'd see in BGC or Alabang — sharp angles, exposed concrete accents, full-height windows, and a deliberate restraint that signals quality without shouting it.

## The Location Advantage: Ciudad Regina, Quezon City

Real estate value starts and ends with location, and Ciudad Regina punches well above its price point. It's a **secure, gated village** in Quezon City — not a converted subdivision, not a barangay road development. The project sits within a private village, which means controlled access, a quieter environment, and the kind of address that holds its value regardless of what's happening in the broader market.

The connectivity from here is genuinely strong. Within roughly 20 to 25 minutes, you can reach **Ever Gotesco** and **UP Town Center**. SM North EDSA is about 31 minutes away, SM Fairview around 39. For medical needs, Diliman Doctors Hospital is approximately 44 minutes away. Schools like Diliman Preparatory School (Commonwealth Campus) are about 32 minutes away.

## Why Two Units Is a Feature, Not a Limitation

In a typical subdivision, you share a village with hundreds — sometimes thousands — of other families. At Esquare Estates Commonwealth, you share a community with exactly one other household. That means less foot traffic, less noise, a shared gate that actually functions as a gate, and a genuine sense of privacy that most gated communities only promise.

For buyers who want the security and prestige of a village address without sacrificing privacy, this kind of micro-enclave is increasingly rare in Metro Manila.
